Orange Sleep · Privacy policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy applies to Orange Sleep for iOS 1.0. The app plays offline soundscapes and music. No registration or sign-in is required.

Local preferences

The app saves favorites, the last selected sound, volume and language preference on your device to restore your settings. The app does not upload these preferences to a server. You can change them in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data.

Data collection and tracking

The app does not collect names, email addresses, location, contacts, advertising identifiers or listening history. It includes no advertising, tracking or third-party analytics services, and does not sell or share personal data. All sounds and cover images are included in the installation; playback does not connect to an audio server.

Apple may process App Store transactions or system diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies. These services are provided by Apple. Orange Sleep does not receive your payment card details in the app.

System audio

The app uses system audio services for background playback and displays the current sound and playback controls on the Lock Screen and in Control Center. It does not access your microphone or record your voice.

Policy updates

This policy will be updated if a future version changes how data is handled. It does not describe features that have not been released.
