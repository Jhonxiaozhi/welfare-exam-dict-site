Orange Sleep · Support

Tap a sound to play. Open the player to adjust volume or choose a 15, 30 or 60 minute timer. Pausing also pauses the countdown. Changing sounds keeps the remaining timer. Tap a heart to save a favorite. Tap the globe on the home screen to change language or follow the device language. If none of your preferred device languages are supported, the app uses English.

If audio stops, check the app volume and output device, then tap Play. Reopen the app if needed. The timer fades during its final three seconds.

For help, email jhonliujing@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version and a description. Do not include passwords or payment details.
